Surplus shirts, unexpected discoveries.

Our idea

SurpTees gives surplus concert merchandise a second life. Customers choose a price tier and size, then receive a tee selected from available inventory.

The surprise

The design, artist, and color are intentionally unknown until your package arrives. Every order is a small piece of music history with a little mystery.
