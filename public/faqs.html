FAQs

Everything you need to know about the surprise.

What exactly am I guaranteed?

You are guaranteed one genuine surplus concert t-shirt for $4. The artist, design, color, and exact shirt are intentionally unknown.

How do I get my order?

Orders are for in-store pickup at The Smoke Shop, 638 Walton Avenue, Bronx, NY.

Is shipping available?

Shipping coming soon. Shipping is not currently offered at checkout.

Can I return or exchange it?

All purchases are final. Because inventory is random and each order is a one-off purchase, we do not offer refunds, returns, or exchanges.

Who do I contact if something goes wrong?

Email hello@surptees.com with your order number and a description of the issue.

Is the payment secure?

Yes. Payment is handled on Stripe-hosted Checkout, so SurpTees does not handle or store your full card number.
