FAQs

Everything you need to know about the surprise.

What exactly am I guaranteed?

You are guaranteed one genuine surplus concert t-shirt in the price tier and size you select. The artist, design, color, and exact shirt are intentionally unknown.

When will it ship?

Orders typically ship within 3-5 business days. You will receive tracking by email when your package leaves our facility.

How much is shipping?

Shipping is calculated and shown at Stripe Checkout before you pay. Rates vary by destination and service.

Can I return/exchange it?

Contact us within 30 days of delivery for return or exchange eligibility. Items must be unworn and unwashed. See Returns & Exchanges for details.

What if my size isn't available?

Only sizes currently offered in the carousel can be ordered. If your preferred size is unavailable, check back later or contact support.

Who do I contact if something goes wrong?

Email hello@surptees.com with your order number and a description of the issue.

Is the payment secure?

Yes. Payment is handled on Stripe-hosted Checkout, so SurpTees does not handle or store your full card number.
